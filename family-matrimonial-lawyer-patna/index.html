Family matters handled with discretion, and with a clear line between the legal question and the personal one.

Overview

The Practice

We act in matrimonial, maintenance, custody and domestic violence matters before the Family Court, the Magistrate courts and the Patna High Court. These cases involve people who will remain connected long after the case ends, and we conduct them with that in mind.

Where settlement or mediation is realistic, it is pursued seriously before a contested hearing. Where it is not, the case is prepared fully for trial.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is mediation compulsory?

Family Courts usually refer parties to counselling or mediation. The chambers treats it as a real opportunity to settle rather than a formality.

Are these matters kept confidential?

Yes. Family matters are handled with strict confidentiality, and proceedings may be held in camera where the law allows.

Do you act for husbands as well as wives?

Yes. The chambers acts for either side, depending on the matter.

Can the six-month waiting period for mutual divorce be waived?

The court has discretion to waive it in suitable cases where the parties have genuinely separated and settled all issues.

What is considered in a custody case?

The welfare of the child is the main consideration, above the rights claimed by either parent.
