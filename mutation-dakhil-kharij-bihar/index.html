Mutation (Dakhil-Kharij) & Land Records in Bihar

Getting your name onto the land records, and correcting them when they are wrong.

Overview

The Practice

After you buy or inherit land in Bihar, the revenue records must be updated to show you as the holder. Without a current jamabandi entry, paying rent, obtaining an LPC, taking a loan or selling the land becomes difficult.

We prepare mutation applications, appear before the Circle Officer when objections are filed, and take appeals before the DCLR and higher revenue authorities. Where an order is illegal, we challenge it before the Patna High Court.

The mutation journey

From Application to Jamabandi

Every stage, including what happens when someone objects or the application is rejected.

1

Document check

Registered deed, the previous holder’s jamabandi details, rent receipts and identity documents are collected and checked.

2

Online application

The mutation (dakhil-kharij) application is filed on the Bihar Bhumi portal with the documents uploaded.

3

Field inquiry

The Revenue Karmachari and Circle Inspector verify the application and submit a report to the Circle Officer.

4

Objection filed?

A public notice is issued and interested persons may object.

YesFile reply and evidence; hearing before the Circle Officer
5

Circle Officer’s order

The Circle Officer allows or rejects the application.

AllowedJamabandi is created or corrected
6

Appeal before the DCLR

The Deputy Collector Land Reforms hears the appeal on the record.

YesFurther challenge before the Collector; writ before the High Court where the order is illegal
✓

Record updated

Your name appears in the jamabandi. Rent receipts and a Land Possession Certificate can now be obtained in your name.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is dakhil-kharij?

It is the mutation of land records: the name of the previous holder is removed (kharij) and the new holder is entered (dakhil) in the jamabandi.

My mutation was rejected. What now?

An appeal lies against the Circle Officer’s order. The time limit is short, so bring the order and your documents as soon as possible.

Does mutation decide ownership?

No. Mutation is a fiscal record for revenue purposes. Title is decided by the civil court on the basis of the documents. But an up-to-date record is important in practice.

Can errors in an old jamabandi be corrected?

Yes, through the correction process on the portal or by application before the revenue authorities, supported by documents.

Do you appear before the revenue courts in person?

Yes. Appearances before the Circle Officer, DCLR and Collector are part of the practice.
