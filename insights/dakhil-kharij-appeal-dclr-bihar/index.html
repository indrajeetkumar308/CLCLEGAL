Mutation Appeal in Bihar: How to Appeal to the DCLR When Dakhil-Kharij Is Rejected

By Pankaj Kumar Choudhary, Advocate, Patna High Court · Choudhary Law Chambers, Patna · 29 October 2026

In Bihar, a mutation (dakhil-kharij) application is decided by the Circle Officer (Anchal Adhikari). Usually it is filed on the Bihar Bhumi portal after a sale, inheritance, gift or partition. When the application is rejected, many landholders simply apply again. That wastes time and often leads to a second rejection on the same ground.

The law gives a specific remedy: an appeal to the Deputy Collector, Land Reforms (DCLR). This article explains when to file it, what to file, and what happens next.

Step 1: Get the rejection order and read the reason

Download or obtain a certified copy of the Circle Officer's order. The reason for rejection decides what the appeal must answer. Common reasons are:

  • a break in the chain of documents (for example, the seller's own name was never mutated)
  • a mismatch in khata, khesra or area between the deed and the jamabandi
  • an objection by a co-sharer or another claimant
  • missing heirship or family-tree papers in an inheritance mutation
  • an incomplete online application, or documents not uploaded

Step 2: File the appeal before the DCLR within 30 days

An appeal against the Circle Officer's order lies before the DCLR of the sub-division under the Bihar Land Mutation Act, 2011. Under Rule 11 of the Bihar Land Mutation Rules, 2012, it must be filed within 30 days from the date of the order.

The appeal memo should contain:

  • the order under appeal and its date
  • a short statement of facts and the chain of title
  • the specific grounds on which the order is wrong
  • the documents relied on
  • the relief sought, usually setting aside the order and directing mutation

Missed the 30 days? Rule 11 allows the DCLR to condone the delay for sufficient reasons. File an application explaining the delay along with the appeal, supported by facts and documents where possible.

Step 3: The hearing

The DCLR issues notice to the other side and calls for the records from the Circle Office. Both sides are then heard. Rule 11 contemplates disposal of the appeal within 30 working days of filing, though in practice it can take longer.

If the appeal is allowed, the case record goes back to the Circle Office. A correction slip is issued and the jamabandi is updated in the revenue records.

Step 4: If the DCLR also rejects

A revision lies before the Collector of the district under the Act and the Rules. The time limit is short, so act promptly. Where an order is without jurisdiction or in breach of natural justice, a writ petition before the Patna High Court may be considered.

Mutation does not decide ownership

Mutation is an entry in the revenue record for fiscal purposes: it shows who pays the rent (lagan). It does not create or take away title. The Supreme Court has held this in Suraj Bhan v. Financial Commissioner, (2007) 6 SCC 186, and in later decisions. If there is a real dispute over title, the remedy is a civil suit, and the mutation follows the civil court's decree.

Documents to keep ready

  • Certified copy of the Circle Officer's order, and the mutation application with its acknowledgement
  • Sale deed, gift deed, partition deed or will, with registration details
  • Previous jamabandi or rent receipt (lagan rasid) in the seller's or ancestor's name
  • Khatiyan, and the Land Possession Certificate (LPC) if issued
  • For inheritance: death certificate, family tree (vanshavali), and the consent of co-sharers where relevant
  • Identity proof of the appellant

Frequently asked questions

Can I just re-apply instead of appealing?

If the rejection was only for a missing upload, a fresh application with the complete documents may work. If the Circle Officer decided a point against you (an objection, or a defect in the chain), an appeal is the proper remedy.

Is the appeal filed online?

The procedure and portal options change from time to time. Check the current practice at the DCLR office or on the Bihar Bhumi portal before filing.

Does a mutation in my name make me the owner?

No. Mutation is a revenue entry. Ownership rests on your title documents, and disputes over title are decided by the civil court.

How long does the appeal take?

The Rules contemplate disposal within 30 working days of filing. Actual time depends on notices, objections and the office’s workload.
