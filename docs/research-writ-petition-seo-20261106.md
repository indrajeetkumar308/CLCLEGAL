## Information gaps and content strategy
- Lead with the searcher's actual question: when can a writ petition help, not merely a definition.
- Give Bihar-specific examples concerning inaction, revenue authorities, licences and public employment.
- Explain five writ types with plain-language names and note that the choice of relief depends on facts.
- Explain Article 226 versus Article 227 and Article 32 without conflating jurisdiction.
- Prominently discuss alternative remedies and Supreme Court exceptions, avoiding any impression that a writ is always preferable to an appeal.
- Provide a checklist, steps, FAQs, and links to official sources.
- Follow BCI Rule 36: educational copy, no ranking claims, promises, testimonials, solicitation, "best lawyer", or call-to-action crafted to obtain business.
- Preserve site architecture: existing article template; native Insight card under Civil Litigation; Civil Litigation and Citizen Guides internal links; visible breadcrumb.
