Website Analytics Notice

Updated: 30 October 2026. This notice concerns optional website usage analytics on clclegal.in. It is not a request to share confidential case information.

Optional analytics

When you select “Allow analytics,” this website loads Google Analytics 4. It can record pages viewed, approximate location and device/browser information and may use analytics cookies. You can decline without losing access to the website.

How we limit data

Our custom page-view event excludes the URL query string and fragment, and advertising personalisation is disabled in our website code. Google Analytics may collect other technical information according to its settings and policies. Do not include confidential case details in website URLs or search boxes.

Your choice

Use the “Analytics settings” control in the website footer to accept, decline or withdraw analytics consent. Withdrawing consent stops new Google Analytics tracking on this browser and attempts to remove locally accessible GA cookies. It does not erase information previously collected by Google.

Third-party services

Google's processing of Analytics data is explained in Google's Privacy Policy. If you choose to open WhatsApp or another external service, that service's separate policies apply.
