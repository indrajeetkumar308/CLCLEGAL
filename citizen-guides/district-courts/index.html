District Courts: Find Case Status Using CNR

Case Status, Hearings & Orders

PUBLIC INFORMATION · NOT LEGAL ADVICE

A citizen guide to India's official eCourts case-search service for district and subordinate courts.

How to use the official service

  1. Find the 16-character alphanumeric CNR number from your case papers, if available.
  2. Visit the official eCourts Services portal and enter CNR without spaces or hyphens, then complete the verification shown.
  3. Read the case status, history and available hearing/order information.
  4. If you do not know CNR, use the portal's other Case Status search options such as case number or party name.

Official government resources

  • eCourts Services — official
  • eCourts official instructions
