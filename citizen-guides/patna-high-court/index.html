Patna High Court: Case Status, Cause List & Orders

Case Status, Cause Lists & Orders

PUBLIC INFORMATION · NOT LEGAL ADVICE

Learn how to find your case status, hearing list and available orders on the official Patna High Court website.

How to use the official service

  1. Open the official Patna High Court e-Sewa page.
  2. Select Case Status and search using the available case number, party name or other supported options.
  3. Use Cause List to check the published listing. A listed case does not guarantee the hearing will proceed.
  4. Search Orders and Judgments for publicly available decisions. For certified copies follow the court's procedure.

Official government resources

  • Patna High Court e-Sewa
  • Patna High Court website

Case status and daily lists are informational; confirm time-sensitive matters with the court registry or the relevant official source. This page is educational information only.
