About the chambers

A litigation practice built on preparation

Choudhary Law Chambers is a Patna-based practice appearing before the Patna High Court, the civil and criminal courts of Bihar, and the revenue authorities. The chambers handles criminal, civil, property, matrimonial, succession and commercial recovery matters.

Every brief is run personally, from the first notice to the final order. Clients receive a clear assessment at the start, the right remedy in the right forum, and an update after every date of hearing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a consultation?

Send an enquiry using the form below, call, or message the chambers on WhatsApp. We will confirm a time for a meeting at the chambers or by phone.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Copies of everything connected to the matter: FIR or complaint, notices, deeds, khatiyan or jamabandi extracts, agreements, bank records and earlier court orders.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the forum, the stage of the matter and the work involved. They are discussed and agreed in writing after the first consultation.

Does contacting the chambers make you my advocate?

No. An advocate–client relationship begins only after the chambers agrees to take up the matter and terms are settled.
