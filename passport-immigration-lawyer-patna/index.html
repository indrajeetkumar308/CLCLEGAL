Immigration & Passport Lawyer in Patna

The Indian legal side of travel, study and work abroad: passports, court permissions, Look Out Circulars and the documents embassies ask for.

Overview

The Practice

Many people in Bihar find that a pending case, a family dispute or a paperwork error stands between them and a passport, a visa or a job abroad. We handle the Indian legal side of these problems: passport refusals and impoundings, court permission to travel while a case is pending, challenges to Look Out Circulars, and the affidavits, certificates and corrections that visa applications need.

Advice on the immigration law of a foreign country is given by practitioners licensed in that country. Where needed, we coordinate with them while handling the Indian proceedings and documents ourselves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a passport if a criminal case is pending against me?

A pending case is a ground on which a passport may be refused, but the court dealing with the case can permit its issue. The application is made in that court with the case details.

Can I travel abroad while on bail?

Only with the court’s permission if your bail conditions restrict travel. The court may impose conditions such as a travel itinerary, an undertaking to return, or a security deposit.

How do I know if a Look Out Circular has been issued against me?

LOCs are not usually served on the person. Often they come to light only at the airport. We can advise on how to find out and how to challenge one.

Do you advise on foreign visa rules?

No. Foreign immigration law is advised on by practitioners licensed in that country. We handle the Indian legal steps and documents, and coordinate with them where needed.

An agent took money for a job abroad and disappeared. What can I do?

A complaint of cheating can be filed with the police or before the Magistrate, and civil proceedings can be taken to recover the money. Keep all receipts, messages and documents given by the agent.
