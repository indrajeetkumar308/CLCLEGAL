Property & Title Advocate in Patna

Counsel through the whole life of a property: checks before you buy, the documents you sign, and the disputes that may follow.

Overview

The Practice

We advise individuals, families and institutions on land and buildings in Bihar. Much of the work is title verification: tracing ownership through khatiyan, registered deeds, jamabandi entries and rent receipts before money changes hands.

Each stage of a property’s life is treated as a separate piece of work with its own documents: due diligence before purchase, the transaction itself, compliance afterwards, and disputes if they arise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a title search necessary before buying land?

Strongly advised. Most disputes after purchase could have been spotted by checking the chain of title, the revenue records and pending litigation before the sale.

What records do you check in Bihar?

Usually the survey khatiyan, registered deeds in the chain, the jamabandi (Register II) entry, recent rent receipts and, where available, the Land Possession Certificate and map records.

Do you handle family property disputes?

Yes, including partition, family settlements and disputes between heirs.

Do you draft deeds as well as litigate?

Yes. Deeds drafted with the likely disputes in mind are the best protection against litigation later.

Can you help with builder disputes?

Yes, including disputes over delayed possession and the terms of allotment and development agreements.
